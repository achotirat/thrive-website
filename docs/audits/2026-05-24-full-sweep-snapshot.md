## Executive Summary

**Pages audited:** 22 Tier A + 63 Tier B = 85 pages total

**Tier B average:** 6.2/10

- `/blog/ashwagandha` (4.7) — no FAQ, no doctor attribution, sparse citations

**Universal site-wide issues (affect all or most pages):**

2. `hreflang` (th-TH, en, x-default) missing on all 63 blog pages + 5 Tier A pages
3. No `FAQPage` JSON-LD on any blog page (Schema: 1/10 across all Tier B)
4. No `loading="lazy"` on most images site-wide
5. PDPA/consent notice missing from all blog pages
6. Logo `<img>` missing `alt` text in header/footer template
