# English and Simplified Chinese: first release

## Routes

Thai remains at the current URLs. English uses `/en/` and Simplified Chinese uses `/zh-cn/`. Each language has these seven pages:

- Home
- About
- Contact
- IV Drip
- Health Check-ups
- HBOT
- Mental Health

The appointment confirmation page is also translated but excluded from indexing.

## Content and behavior

The English and Chinese pages are independently written versions of the main page topics. They preserve the clinic's contact details, hours, location, service categories, and appointment flow. Each page now includes audience guidance, a step-by-step visit or service journey, practical questions to confirm before booking, and additional FAQs. They do not reproduce every programme description on the longer Thai pages. The introductory copy is in `astro/src/i18n/pages.ts`; the expanded content and photo mapping are in `astro/src/i18n/enhancements.ts`.

The photos come from assets already used in this repository: clinic interior (`thrive-clinic-home.jpg`), reception (`about-clinic.jpg`), Dr. Chanakan's profile photo, IV drip room and care photo, and the check-up consultation room. The HBOT and mental health pages use clinic photographs. No verified photo of the current HBOT chamber was found among the examined assets, so those pages explicitly say that their photographs show the clinic, not the equipment. No patient photo is used to imply a mental health outcome.

The language switcher links between matching translated pages. It is omitted on Thai pages that do not yet have English and Chinese equivalents. SEO alternates are emitted only for translated route groups; untranslated pages keep their Thai canonical. The static sitemap is generated by Astro, and the old checked-in sitemap was removed.

## Before publishing

- Have a qualified Thai/English and Thai/Chinese reviewer check the translated medical wording, service names, and consent text.
- Confirm whether Chinese-speaking appointment support is available. The site text offers an enquiry form and LINE contact, without promising Chinese-speaking staff.
- Confirm that existing clinic and service photographs may be reused on English and Chinese pages, including the IV drip care photo.
- Supply a current, approved HBOT chamber photograph if the service page should show the equipment itself.
- Review the final pages at desktop and mobile sizes on the Netlify deploy preview.

Sanity blog posts, individual check-up programmes, other service pages, quizzes, and ad landing pages remain Thai until translated content is ready. Do not emit language alternates to those pages yet.
