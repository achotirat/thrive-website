# English and Simplified Chinese: first release

## Routes

Thai remains at the current URLs. English uses `/en/` and Simplified Chinese uses `/zh-cn/`. Each language has these seven pages:

- Home
- About
- Contact
- IV Drip
- Health Check-ups
- HBOT
- Mental Health

The appointment confirmation page is also translated but excluded from indexing.

## Content and behavior

The English and Chinese pages are concise, independently written versions of the main page topics. They preserve the clinic's contact details, hours, location, service categories, and appointment flow. They do not reproduce every section or every programme description on the longer Thai pages. The source copy is in `astro/src/i18n/pages.ts` so each language can be reviewed and expanded without duplicating route templates.

The language switcher links between matching translated pages. It is omitted on Thai pages that do not yet have English and Chinese equivalents. SEO alternates are emitted only for translated route groups; untranslated pages keep their Thai canonical. The static sitemap is generated by Astro, and the old checked-in sitemap was removed.

## Before publishing

- Have a qualified Thai/English and Thai/Chinese reviewer check the translated medical wording, service names, and consent text.
- Confirm whether Chinese-speaking appointment support is available. The site text offers an enquiry form and LINE contact, without promising Chinese-speaking staff.
- Review the final pages at desktop and mobile sizes on the Netlify deploy preview.

Sanity blog posts, individual check-up programmes, other service pages, quizzes, and ad landing pages remain Thai until translated content is ready. Do not emit language alternates to those pages yet.
