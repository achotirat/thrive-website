# Wix Renewal Checklist

Phase 0 technical foundation is complete, but Wix renewal is a billing/account action that must be completed by the account owner.

## Goal

Keep the existing Wix production site available through at least **December 2026** as a safety buffer while the Astro site is rebuilt and cut over.

## Steps

1. Log in to the Wix account that owns `www.thrivewellnessth.com`.
2. Go to billing / premium subscriptions.
3. Confirm the current plan expiration date.
4. Renew or extend the plan through December 2026 or later.
5. Confirm the domain connection for `www.thrivewellnessth.com` remains unchanged.
6. Save the invoice/receipt in the clinic admin records.
7. Update `PHASE0_STATUS.md` after renewal is confirmed.

## Do Not Change Yet

- Do not point `www.thrivewellnessth.com` to Netlify yet.
- Do not cancel Wix before Phase 7 cutover and monitoring are complete.
- Do not remove existing Wix tracking until GA4/GTM parity is verified on Astro.
