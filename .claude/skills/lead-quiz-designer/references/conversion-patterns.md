# Lead Quiz Conversion Patterns

## Question Order

1. **Recognition question**
   - "Which symptom/problem feels closest to you right now?"
   - Purpose: create relevance and segment by pain.

2. **Severity question**
   - "How often does this happen?"
   - Purpose: qualify urgency.

3. **Duration question**
   - "How long has this been going on?"
   - Purpose: distinguish casual curiosity from real demand.

4. **Prior attempts question**
   - "What have you already tried?"
   - Purpose: reveal sophistication and readiness.

5. **Goal question**
   - "What outcome matters most?"
   - Purpose: route offer angle and nurture copy.

6. **Readiness question**
   - "When would you like help?"
   - Purpose: qualify intent without sounding salesy.

7. **Contact capture**
   - "Where should we send your result / next-step recommendation?"
   - Purpose: convert after value is established.

## Result Types

Use 3-5 result categories:

- **Low urgency / education**: nurture with guide/checklist.
- **Moderate fit / consult**: recommend consultation or short call.
- **High urgency / high fit**: recommend direct booking or LINE follow-up.
- **Wrong fit / safety redirect**: suggest alternative service or professional help.
- **Premium fit**: invite consult/package discussion when budget/readiness is clear.

## Scoring Dimensions

Recommended baseline:

- `fit`: how well the service matches the visitor's problem.
- `urgency`: severity, duration, disruption.
- `intent`: readiness to talk/book/buy.

Optional dimensions:

- `sleep`
- `stress`
- `skin`
- `toxicity`
- `recovery`
- `energy`
- `hormone`
- `digestive`
- `budget`

## Copy Rules

Good:

- "Your answers suggest..."
- "This pattern may be related to..."
- "A clinician can help determine..."
- "Recommended next step..."

Avoid:

- "You have..."
- "This proves..."
- "Cure"
- "Guaranteed"
- "Permanent results"
- "No risk"

## Nurture Segment Naming

Use stable, lowercase, hyphenated names:

- `{service}-{primary-pain}`
- `{service}-{readiness}`
- `{service}-{result}`

Examples:

- `mental-sleep`
- `mental-burnout`
- `iv-skin-glow`
- `iv-fatigue`
- `chelation-toxic-load`
- `hbot-recovery`
- `food-intolerance-digestive`
